Questions & Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding online giving security, tax receipts, and payment options.

Are my donations tax-deductible?

Yes. Christ Presbyterian Church is a registered 501(c)(3) religious non-profit organization. All charitable gifts are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by U.S. law.

When do I receive my tax statement?

Annual giving statements summarizing all gifts made during the previous calendar year are distributed every January via email and mail for tax filing purposes.

Can I set up or modify recurring giving?

Yes. When giving online, you can choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly automated giving. You can adjust the amount, pause, or update payment methods anytime through the donor portal.

What are the transaction fees?

Credit and debit card gifts incur a typical processing fee (approx. 2-3%). If you wish to minimize fees so 100% of your gift reaches ministry, we recommend selecting ACH bank account transfer.

Have Questions About Giving?

Our church office and financial secretary are happy to assist with receipts, designated gifts, or updating recurring pledges.
