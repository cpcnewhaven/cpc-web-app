What to expect

You don’t need to know the songs, the liturgy, or anyone in the room. Our bulletin guides you through the service, and our welcome team is happy to help.

Wear what’s comfortable

You’ll see everything from jeans and t-shirts to jackets and bowties.

Guided worship

A printed bulletin walks you through every part of the service and explains why we do it.

Children belong here

Children are welcome in worship. Carefully managed childcare is also available when your family needs it.

Stay for lunch

{% if show_fellowship %}Our shared meal is free for visitors and a relaxed way to meet people.{% else %}Ask our welcome team about the next community meal.{% endif %}

A safe place for real people

People arrive at CPC from many places in their spiritual journeys—curious about Christianity, returning to church, or following Christ for years. We want to be a community where you can be known honestly and encounter the grace of God in Jesus.

There is no pressure to have everything figured out. Come, observe, participate as you are comfortable, and let us know how we can serve you.
