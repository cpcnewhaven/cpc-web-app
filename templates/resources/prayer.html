A Guide to Personal Prayer: The A.C.T.S. Method

When entering into daily prayer, this historic biblical framework helps shape our conversation with God so that adoration precedes supplication.

A
Adoration

Praising God for who He is — His holiness, majesty, mercy, grace, wisdom, and steadfast love, independent of our circumstances.

C
Confession

Acknowledging our sins, idols, and failures before a forgiving Father, claiming the righteousness of Christ and the cleansing of the cross.

T
Thanksgiving

Giving thanks for specific blessings: daily bread, salvation, family, answered prayers, and trials through which God produces endurance.

S
Supplication

Bringing our requests, intercessions for the sick, burdens for the city, and petitions for spiritual growth boldly before the throne of grace.

Historic & Liturgical Prayers

Treasures of the Christian tradition to anchor your morning and evening devotions.

The Lord's Prayer (Matthew 6:9-13):
“Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name. Your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. For yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory, forever. Amen.”

A Morning Collect for Grace

“O Lord, our heavenly Father, almighty and everlasting God, who has safely brought us to the beginning of this day: Defend us in the same with your mighty power; and grant that this day we fall into no sin, neither run into any kind of danger; but that all our doings, being ordered by your governance, may be righteous in your sight; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.”
