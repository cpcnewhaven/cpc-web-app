How to Study Scripture: The Inductive Method

A simple, reproducible three-step approach used in CPC LifeGroups and personal devotions to let God's Word speak clearly.

1
Observation

What does the text say? Notice the context, key words, repeated phrases, verb tenses, contrasts, and connecting words (e.g. “therefore”, “because”, “so that”).

2
Interpretation

What does the text mean? What was the author's intended message to the original audience? How does this passage connect to the Gospel and the whole storyline of Scripture?

3
Application

How should we respond? What truth should we believe? What sin should we repent of? How does this call us to love God, neighbor, and city in Christ?
