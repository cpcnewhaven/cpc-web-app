Serving Teams at CPC New Haven

The ministry of Christ Presbyterian Church flourishes through the faithful participation of our members and attendees. Explore our core teams below.

Monthly Rotation • Sundays 9:45 AM

Hospitality & Welcome Desk

Welcome visitors at the front doors, answer questions at the foyer desk, distribute worship bulletins, and set up our Sunday coffee and fellowship hour.

Bi-Weekly or Monthly • Background Check Required

Children's Ministry & Nursery

Serve our covenant kids as nursery caregivers, Sunday school teachers, or classroom assistants. Curriculum and training materials are fully provided.

Flexible Schedule • Weekdays or Weekends

Mercy & City Outreach

Partner with the CPC Diaconate to serve vulnerable neighbors in Greater New Haven through food drives, Christian Community Action support, and mercy care.

Rehearsals + Sunday Services

Music & Choir

Lead the congregation in historic hymns, Psalms, and modern spiritual songs through vocals, instrumental accompaniments, and seasonal choral offerings.

Monthly Rotation • Sundays 9:30 AM

Audio Engineering & Livestream Tech

Operate the sound mixing board, manage live microphones, run slides, and direct the YouTube livestream cameras to serve homebound saints and visitors.

Quarterly Work Days + Periodic Projects

Building Stewardship & Work Days

Help maintain our historic 135 Whitney building and grounds through seasonal cleanup work days, interior repairs, and sanctuary care.

Volunteer Interest Form

Let us know where you feel called to serve or explore. A ministry team leader will follow up with details and training steps.
