The Westminster Standards & Ecumenical Creeds

As a congregation in the Presbyterian Church in America (PCA), our officers subscribe to the historic Westminster Standards as containing the system of doctrine taught in Holy Scripture.

Historical Landmark • 1647

The Westminster Confession of Faith

Thirty-three robust chapters summarizing biblical doctrine on Holy Scripture, the Trinity, God's Eternal Decree, Creation, Providence, the Fall, the Covenant of Grace, Christ the Mediator, Justification, Sanctification, the Church, and the Sacraments.

Catechetical Instruction • 1647

The Shorter Catechism

107 concise questions and answers designed for educating families, youth, and new believers. Famous for Question 1: “What is the chief end of man? Man's chief end is to glorify God, and to enjoy him forever.”

Apostles' & Nicene Creeds

Presbyterian Church Government & Leadership

We believe the biblical model of church leadership is connectional and collegial — governed by biblically qualified elders and served by deacons.

The CPC Session (ruling elders and teaching elders/pastors) oversees the spiritual health, doctrine, and worship of the flock, while the Diaconate leads our ministries of mercy, hospitality, and stewardship. Training and reading materials are provided for prospective officers and ministry leaders throughout the church year.
