Sunday School Classes & Age Groups

Sundays at 9:30 AM (during the academic year) prior to our 10:30 AM worship service. Clean, safe classrooms with vetted volunteer teachers.

Ages 0 – 2 Years

Nursery

Loving care in a clean nursery equipped with age-appropriate toys, gentle volunteers, and parent-pager support during both Sunday school and worship.

Ages 3 – 5 Years

Preschool & Kindergarten

Introductory stories from the Old and New Testaments, singing Scripture songs, simple catechism memory, and creative crafts highlighting God's faithfulness.

Grades 1 – 3

Early Elementary

Exploring the unfolding story of redemption in Jesus through Show Me Jesus materials, learning the books of the Bible, and memorizing Scripture verses.

Grades 4 – 6

Upper Elementary

Deeper study of biblical narrative, understanding the covenants, reading the Psalms and Proverbs, and memorizing the Westminster Shorter Catechism.

Grades 7 – 12

Youth Sunday School

Engaging apologetics, Christian worldview, theological foundations, and adolescent discipleship led by pastoral staff and youth leaders.

Curriculum Scope: Gospel-Centered Redemptive Story

We partner with Great Commission Publications (GCP) to ensure that every lesson points children toward Jesus Christ as the Promised Savior, rather than merely moralistic stories.

The curriculum is designed on a multi-year cycle covering both Old and New Testaments, building biblical literacy, theological coherence, and gospel assurance in young hearts.

The First Catechism: Foundations for Young Hearts

A classic catechetical tool simplified for children to instill foundational Christian truth through questions and answers.

Q1. Who made you?

A: God.

Q2. What else did God make?

A: God made all things.

Q3. Why did God make you and all things?

A: For His own glory.

Q4. How can you glorify God?

A: By loving Him and doing what He commands.

Q5. Why ought you to glorify God?

A: Because He made me and takes care of me.

Q6. Are there more gods than one?

A: There is only one God.

Q7. In how many persons does this one God exist?

A: In three persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

Q8. Who is the Redeemer of God's elect?

A: The only Redeemer is the Lord Jesus Christ.

A Simple 15-Minute Family Worship Rhythm

Family worship does not need to be intimidating or complicated. A simple three-part rhythm around the dinner table or before bed:

1. Read (5 Minutes)

Read a short chapter or passage from Scripture (or the Jesus Storybook Bible for younger kids). Ask one simple question about what was read.

2. Pray (5 Minutes)

Pray together as a family. Give thanks for the day, pray for your church, teachers, friends, and family needs. Let children pray brief sentences.

3. Sing or Catechize (5 Minutes)

Sing a verse of a hymn (like Amazing Grace or the Doxology) or practice 2–3 questions from the First Catechism together.
