Who we are

Our Total Christ Spirituality

Our hope is in Christ alone

We want our life together to be centered on Christ - his work, not ours, as the basis of our relationship to God and one another. Our hope is to follow Jesus with a whole-church, whole-life faith.

The Word was made flesh, and dwelled among us; to that flesh is joined the church, and there is made total Christ, both head and body.

— St. Augustine, 5th-century pastor-theologian

Our distinctives

The Five Marks

How we try to live and worship together

Gospel-Centered

The gospel keeps us humble because salvation is a gift, and confident because Christ has done the saving work for us. Everything we do starts there.

Missional

God sends his people into the world, so we try to be open, welcoming, and purposeful in showing our neighbors who Jesus is.

Confessional

We trust the Bible and the historic Westminster Standards as a faithful summary of what we believe. They help us stay rooted in God's Word.

Sacramental

We want our worship to be shaped by the gospel, centered on Christ, and expressed with reverence and joy, including weekly communion.

Communal

Following Jesus means belonging to his church. We aim to live with humility, care, and encouragement for one another.

Our faith

What We Believe

The doctrines at the heart of who we are

We believe the Bible is God's true and trustworthy Word, speaking clearly and powerfully to every part of our lives. We believe salvation comes through faith in Jesus Christ alone. Christ's death and resurrection reconcile us to God, and nothing we do can earn or add to that gift. We believe God is sovereign over all creation and history, and that Christ will one day make all things new.

We believe the Holy Spirit lives in believers, helps us grow, and equips us to serve in God's kingdom. We believe the church is Christ's body, called to make disciples, love one another, and show the world what it means to follow Jesus. We believe we are called to pursue holiness, live out the gospel in community, and join God's mission in the world.

We believe believers will spend eternity in God's presence in the new heavens and new earth, and that Christ will return in glory to complete God's kingdom. Until that day, we are committed to worshiping God, growing in faith, and extending God's love and justice to our community and the world.
