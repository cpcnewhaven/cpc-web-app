Worship Slides Wizard

Follow four simple steps to convert your Sunday bulletin into a standardized Google Slides presentation, with optional Planning Center song lyric piping.

✦ How does this wizard work behind the scenes? Click to learn more ▾
1
Smart Bulletin Parser

Reads your Google Doc bulletin (or pasted text), extracting the Order of Worship into liturgical sections (Call to Worship, Songs, Prayers, Scripture, Sermon, Announcements).

2
Planning Center Lyric Piping

Matches songs in the bulletin with your Planning Center Services plan, pulling the exact lyrics and arrangements into your slides automatically.

3
Automatic Slide Chunking

Song verses and readings are automatically split into 3–5 line slide blocks with verse labels so projection text remains large, readable, and comfortable for the congregation.

4
Standardized Template Cloner

Clones your church’s master Google Slides template, preserving typography, colors, and background designs, then populates every slide ready in Google Drive.
